Business context

Ember & Oak is a fictional, family-run, 60-cover restaurant. As its Chief Technology Officer, my job is to modernise how it deals with customers without adding cost or extra work for staff.

Right now, bookings come in by phone during the busiest hours, the menu is a PDF that's hard to read on a phone, there's no simple way to hear what customers think, and offers only reach people who walk past.

Digital transformation goals

  • Customer engagement: loyalty club, promotions and social media
  • Operational efficiency: fewer booking calls at peak times
  • Customer experience: a mobile-friendly menu with dietary filters
  • Communication: direct feedback to the manager

Privacy, security and cookies

        

          

            
What we collect

            
Only what you type into the form: name, email, optional phone, and booking or feedback details. We collect the minimum needed to reply (UK GDPR data minimisation).

          

          

            
How it's used

            
Your details are sent by your own email app, straight to the restaurant. This website stores nothing. We use them only to answer you and delete booking emails after 12 months.

          

          

            
Your rights

            
You can ask to see, correct or delete your data at any time by emailing us. Loyalty Club members can leave by replying "unsubscribe".

          

          

            
Cookies

            
There are no tracking or advertising cookies. Your choice on the cookie notice is saved in your browser's local storage so it doesn't keep asking. 

          

          

            
Security

            
The site is served over HTTPS by GitHub Pages. The form checks what you type before building the email, and no payment details are ever asked for.

          

        

      

    


    
    

      

        
Design choices

        

          

            Each feature and the business problem it solves
            
              
FeatureBusiness problemWhy this approach

            
            
              
Filterable menuThe PDF menu was hard to read on phonesPlain HTML text loads fast, can be found by search engines and is read out by screen readers

              
Email-generating formBooking calls tie up staff at peak timesNo database or monthly booking-software fee; staff reply from the inbox they already use

              
Feedback and ratingNo direct way to hear from customersComplaints reach the manager privately before they turn into public reviews

              
Loyalty ClubCustomers don't have a reason to come backA simple sign-up builds an opt-in mailing list for promotions

              
"Open now" statusCustomers ring to ask if the restaurant is openJavaScript checks the opening hours against the visitor's clock

              
Social linksOffers only reach passing tradeFacebook, Instagram and X reach the local student and family market

              
Mobile-first, accessible layoutMost diners search on their phoneLarge tap targets, real labels, strong contrast, keyboard friendly

              
Basic SEOThe restaurant is hard to find on GoogleA clear page title, meta description, proper headings and alt text on images
